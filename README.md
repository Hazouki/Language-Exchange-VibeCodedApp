# Language-Exchange-VibeCodedApp
This prompt tells the AI to pretend to be a web developer who solves problems. You give it a problem, and it designs a website to fix that problem.

Why use it?
When you tell the AI to play a role, its answer becomes more focused and useful. Instead of a general answer, you get clear ideas like what pages the website needs, what features to add, and how people will use it.

What can you learn?
Next time you get a problem card, you can:

Read the problem well first. Who has the problem? What do they need?
Give the AI more details, like who will use the website and what features you want. More details give a better design.
Treat the AI's answer as a first draft. Check it, ask more questions, and improve it step by step.

This way you learn how to plan a website and how to guide AI to help you.
