Kept
solution concept + working prototype
Language exchange that shows up

Same partner. Same hour. Every week.

Kept matches you on when you're free before what you speak, binds you both to a short weekly pact, and pairs people who keep their word with each other.

The problem: someone learning a new language needs a regular conversation partner, while most exchange apps match them with people who never show up.

Diagnosis

Why partners disappear, and the fix for each cause

Cause

Matching ignores time

You match on language, then find out your partner is asleep whenever you're free. The chat fades out.

Fix

Schedule-first matching

You mark your free weekly hours. You only see people whose hours overlap yours, shown in both time zones.

Cause

Nothing is at stake

An open-ended chat costs nothing to ignore, and dozens of matches make each one feel disposable.

Fix

One partner, one weekly pact

A 4-week agreement on a fixed slot. You have one active partner at a time. Each session needs a confirm 24 hours before.

Cause

Reliable and flaky users are mixed

The people who do show up keep getting matched with people who don't, and they quit first.

Fix

Reliability record and bands

Every profile shows sessions kept out of sessions scheduled. Matching only pairs people in the same reliability band.

Cause

Sessions feel one-sided

One person ends up teaching for an hour and gets nothing back, so they stop coming.

Fix

A 15 / 15 session room

A built-in timer splits each call evenly between both languages, with topic prompts so nobody has to plan.

Cause

One no-show breaks the habit

A missed week turns into a missed month, and the learner has to start searching again.

Fix

Standby cover and auto-rematch

A backup learner from the standby pool covers a missed slot. Two no-shows end the pact and you're rematched automatically.

Building it for real

What the MVP needs

Data and matching

  • Store availability as UTC minute-of-week ranges so overlap is a set intersection.
  • Reliability = kept ÷ scheduled over the last 12 sessions. New users start in a trial band.
  • A nightly job proposes matches, ranked by band, then overlap hours.

Stack and calls

  • Next.js + Postgres (Supabase for auth and row-level security).
  • Video in-browser through LiveKit or Daily, so the timer and prompts sit beside the call.
  • Reminders by push and email at 24 h (confirm) and 15 min (join link).
  • Attendance is logged automatically from who joined the room.

The number to watch

Week-4 show-up

The share of pacts where both people attend session 4. That is the habit forming. Track it next to standby fill rate and time to rematch.
